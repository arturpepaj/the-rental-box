Manager’s Desk

Yardi or AppFolio data: the Yardi Virtuoso connector reads your Yardi Voyager data in Claude with your normal Yardi login. It needs Yardi’s Virtuoso Enterprise package. Ask IT or your Yardi administrator to turn it on.
This also works with AppFolio: connect AppFolio Realm-X with your AppFolio login.
Connect both in claude.ai Settings → Connectors.
